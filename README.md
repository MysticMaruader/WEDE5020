Netcampus Group
Brief History
Netcampus Group is a South African Information and Communications Technology (ICT) training and technology solutions organisation. The organisation has more than two decades of experience in ICT training and has developed a presence across South Africa.
According to the organisation's official website, Netcampus has offices in Centurion, Fourways and Cape Town and provides training across all nine provinces. The organisation works with school leavers, individuals, professionals, businesses, government and other organisations.
Netcampus provides vendor-authorised technology training involving major technology providers such as Microsoft, AWS, Google and CompTIA. Its website currently lists hundreds of available courses and provides training schedules and certification-related services.
Mission
Netcampus's mission is to provide diverse technology training products, customised solutions and innovative training technologies that empower people with accessible and practical technology skills.
Vision
Netcampus aims to be a frontrunner in ICT training by empowering individuals to unlock their full potential and achieve excellence through innovative education and support.

Values
To support its mission and vision, Netcampus is guided by the following values:
•	Innovation – Continuously adopting new technologies and teaching methods to stay ahead in ICT education.
•	Accessibility – Ensuring training opportunities are available to diverse groups, including school leavers, professionals, and organisations.
•	Integrity – Delivering training with honesty, transparency, and professionalism.
•	Excellence – Striving for the highest standards in training delivery and learner support.
•	Collaboration – Building strong partnerships with technology vendors, businesses, and government institutions.
•	Empowerment – Equipping learners with practical skills that enhance employability and career growth.

Target Audience
The proposed website will target:
•	School leavers interested in technology careers.
•	Students and graduates.
•	IT professionals.
•	Individuals looking to obtain technology certifications.
•	Businesses requiring employee training.
•	Government organisations.
•	NGOs and other organisations requiring ICT skills development.
Website Goals and Objectives: Goals
The main goal of the proposed website is to provide users with clear, accessible and organised information about Netcampus, its services, courses and physical locations.
Objectives
The website objectives are as follow:
•	Increase awareness of Netcampus and its ICT training services.
•	Make information about courses easier to access.
•	Provide users with clear information about the organisation.
•	Provide easy access to contact information.
•	Allow users to identify Netcampus office locations.
•	Present the organisation's vision, mission and values.
•	Encourage prospective learners and organisations to make enquiries.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The success of the website can be measured using:
•	Number of website visitors.
•	Number of course enquiries.
•	Number of contact form submissions.
•	Number of users viewing course information.
•	Number of users accessing the contact/location page.
•	Number of users returning to the website.
•	Number of potential learners making training enquiries.
