About Us

Who We Are

Netcampus is an Information and Communications Technology training organisation based in South Africa.

The organisation provides technology training and development solutions for individuals, professionals, businesses and organisations.

Netcampus has more than 20 years of experience in ICT training and operates offices in Centurion, Fourways and Cape Town.

Our Vision

To be a frontrunner in ICT training by empowering individuals to unlock their full potential and achieve excellence through innovative education and supports.

Netcampus aims to inspire lifelong learning and contribute to the success of individuals in the continuously evolving technology industry.

Our Mission

Netcampus provides diverse technology training products, customised solutions and innovative training technologies.

The organisation aims to empower people with technology skills that are accessible, practical and relevant to the the changing digital environment.
